What we do

Devdic Herbal is a clinic based in Tajpur, Samastipur, Bihar. We help people living with diabetes manage their daily care through ayurvedic and herbal medicines prescribed by our doctors, together with diet and lifestyle guidance.

Diabetes care with Ayurveda

  • Doctor consultation — chat and video call with our doctors, so you do not have to travel for every follow-up.
  • Personal prescription — your medicines and plan are saved in the app and always available.
  • Medicine reminders — alarms at the right time so a dose is not missed.
  • Order & delivery — order your medicine and track it until it reaches you.
  • Hindi + English — simple to use for every family member.

How it works

  1. Install the Devdic Herbal app (Android) or open it in your browser.
  2. Register with your mobile number.
  3. Talk to our doctor and get your prescription.
  4. Order your medicine and follow the reminders.
Important: Ayurvedic and herbal medicines support overall care and are not a replacement for your existing diabetes treatment. Please do not stop or change any medicine prescribed by your physician without their advice. In an emergency go to the nearest hospital.
