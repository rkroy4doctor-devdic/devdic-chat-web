Privacy Policy

DEVDIC HERBAL (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED ("Devdic Herbal", "we", "us")

Last updated: October 2026. This policy explains how we collect and use information when you use the Devdic Herbal Android app (package: com.devdicherbal.app) and our website.

Information we collect

Account and profile: name, mobile number, age, gender, address, patient ID. Login is by mobile-number OTP.

Health information: symptoms, diabetes/health details, prescriptions, medicine plans and reminders, and reports or photos you choose to share with our doctors.

Orders and payments: orders, billing history, wallet transactions and payment status/reference. We do not receive or store your card number, UPI PIN or bank login.

Messages and media: chat messages with our staff and doctors, and photos, documents or voice recordings you choose to send.

Camera and photos: only when you take or attach a photo (for example a prescription, report or profile picture), or scan a QR code.

Microphone and video calls: only during a voice message or an in-app video consultation, which you start or accept.

Location: approximate or precise location, used to verify the delivery address and, for our staff members, for attendance at their branch. Staff location may also be collected while the app runs in the background to keep attendance reliable.

Device and diagnostics: device model, app version, notification token and crash/error logs, used to deliver notifications and fix problems.

How we use it

To register you as a patient, provide consultations and prescriptions, process orders and deliveries, manage your wallet and payments, send reminders (such as medicine alarms), appointment and order notifications, prevent fraud, improve and fix the app, and meet legal obligations.

Permissions the app may ask for

Notifications, camera, photos/files, microphone, location, exact alarms (medicine reminders), and permission to show an urgent call-style alert for doctor and staff alerts. You can turn any of these off in your phone settings; the related feature will then not work.

Payments

Online payments are processed by Razorpay (our payment gateway) and UPI apps. We receive only the payment status, amount and a transaction reference.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only with service providers needed to run the service: Google Firebase (authentication, database, storage, notifications, crash reporting), Razorpay (payments), delivery partners, and Jitsi (video calls); and when required by law. Your health information is visible only to our doctors and authorised staff.

Security and retention

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS) and stored on secured cloud services. We keep records for as long as needed to provide the service and comply with law (including tax and medical record-keeping).

Your choices and account deletion

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your account and personal data at any time by contacting us below with your registered mobile number. We will delete or anonymise your data within 30 days, except records we must keep by law (for example bills and tax records).

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. Minors may use it only through a parent or guardian.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above.
