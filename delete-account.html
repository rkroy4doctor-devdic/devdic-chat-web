Delete Your Account — DEVDIC HERBAL

This page explains how to request deletion of your account and data for the Devdic Herbal Android app (package: com.devdicherbal.app), published by DEVDIC HERBAL (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED.

How to request deletion

  1. Send a message on WhatsApp or SMS to 8434813523, or an email to devdicherbal@gmail.com.
  2. Write: "Please delete my Devdic Herbal account" and include your name, registered mobile number and patient ID (if you know it).
  3. We will verify that the request is from you (we may call the registered number), and then delete your account.
  4. You will get a confirmation within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile (name, mobile number, address, age, gender)
  • Chat messages, photos, reports and voice messages you shared
  • Medicine reminders and notification tokens
  • Your wallet profile (any unused wallet balance will be settled with you before deletion)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Bills, payment and tax records — kept as required by Indian tax and accounting law (up to 8 years).
  • Prescription and treatment records — kept for the period required for medical record-keeping, in a restricted form that only authorised clinic staff can access.
  • Crash and security logs — kept for a short period and not linked to your profile after deletion.
