Failed or duplicate payments

If money is debited from your account but the wallet recharge or order is not confirmed, or you were charged twice, the amount is refunded to the original payment method within 5-7 working days after verification.

Wallet balance

Wallet balance is meant to be used on Devdic Herbal services and medicines. Refund of unused wallet balance to the bank is handled on request through our support team, subject to verification.

Orders and returns

Medicines can be returned or exchanged only if wrong, damaged or expired at delivery, reported with photos within 48 hours. Approved refunds go to your wallet or original payment method.

Cancellation

Orders can be cancelled before dispatch. After dispatch, cancellation is not possible.
